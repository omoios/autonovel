## ACT I: THE SEDIMENT (0% - 23%)

**Goal:** Establish the vertical tyranny, the cost of magic (Nausea), and the immediate threat of the Drill. Cassian is reactive, preserving history in small ways while hiding from his past.

### Chapter 1: The Rust Belt
*   **POV:** Cassian
*   **Location:** The Slums / The Rust Belt (Edge of the Pit)
*   **StC Beat:** Opening Image
*   **% Mark:** 0-3%
*   **Emotional Arc:** Resignation → Prickling Anxiety
*   **Try-Fail:** Yes-But (Cassian successfully scavenges a pre-Scorch button, BUT the city groans, signaling a sink-event).
*   **Beats:**
    1.  Cassian scavenges "The Fall" — debris from the Spire raining down. He finds a non-essential object (a glass button) and pockets it (Character: Collector).
    2.  Sensory introduction to the Nausea: Cassian’s hand shakes, his skin ripples like water (aging/de-aging). He checks his Chronometer. He is "drifting."
    3.  Introduction of Kait. She tries to steal the button. He catches her. She warns him the "White Coats are sniffing around Sector 4."
    4.  The City groans. A massive tremor. The "Sanitation Drill" kicks into high gear somewhere deep below.
    5.  Cassian looks down into the Pit. The lights of the drill are visible miles below. He knows they are hitting the Basalt Threshold.
*   **Plants:** The glass button (beauty in a dead world), Kait’s rat-talking, the Chronometer counting down.
*   **The Lie:** Reinforced. Cassian believes if he just finds the "big" history (The Queen), his drifting will stop and he’ll be "solid" again.

### Chapter 2: The Audit
*   **POV:** Cassian
*   **Location:** The Mid-Levels (Market)
*   **StC Beat:** Theme Stated
*   **% Mark:** 3-6%
*   **Emotional Arc:** Cautious → Repulsed
*   **Try-Fail:** No-But (Cassian fails to sell his scavenged Chronite without drawing attention, BUT he learns valuable info about the Drill).
*   **Beats:**
    1.  Cassian visits a fence to sell scavenged Chronite shards. The market is tense. "Sanitizer" patrols are increased.
    2.  He sees a public "Sanitation" broadcast. High Auditor Vex speaks: "We consume the past to fuel the future." (Theme stated explicitly).
    3.  Cassian witnesses a "Duster" (child thief) being apprehended by Sanitizers. They don't arrest him; they use a sonic device to *erase* his timeline. He turns to sludge. Cassian is horrified but does nothing (Wound: Joren).
    4.  Kait appears, steals a ration tin, and leads the Sanitizers on a chase, creating a distraction for Cassian to slip away.
*   **Plants:** The Sonic Cannon (Sanitizer weapon), Vex’s rhetoric.
*   **Payoffs:** None yet.
*   **The Lie:** The world is too dangerous to act. Keep your head down, dig your holes.

### Chapter 3: The Orphanage
*   **POV:** Cassian
*   **Location:** The Slums (Mother Otho's)
*   **StC Beat:** Setup
*   **% Mark:** 6-10%
*   **Emotional Arc:** Burdened → Defensive
*   **Try-Fail:** Yes-But (Cassian delivers food to Otho, BUT she confronts him about his deteriorating health).
*   **Beats:**
    1.  Cassian brings "slurry" to Otho’s orphanage. It's meager rations.
    2.  Otho, looking ancient (though 24), examines Cassian. She sees the black ichor bleeding from his pores. "You're digging too deep, Cass."
    3.  Otho reveals the Intel: The Sanitizers are prepping a "Mole" — a mega-drill — to punch through the Basalt Threshold in 48 hours.
    4.  She hands him a "Resonance Map" — a illegal chart of the deep strata showing the Queen’s location. "If you want to save her, you go now. Before the Mole eats her."
    5.  Cassian refuses. "I'm a chronicler, not a suicide runner." He leaves, but takes the map.
*   **Plants:** The Mole (48-hour timer), The Resonance Map.
*   **The Lie:** Cassian claims he's just observing. Otho calls him on his cowardice.

*   **% Mark:** 10-12%

    3.  Joren speaks: "Dig. The dark is cold." The voice is distorted, skipping like a record.

    5.  He looks at the map Otho gave him. The Queen’s chamber overlaps with Joren’s last known coordinates.
*   **Plants:** Joren’s voice as a physical phenomenon in the rock.
*   **The Lie:** Challenged. Cassian thinks he can save Joren if he digs fast enough.

### Chapter 6: The Basalt Door

*   **StC Beat:** Break Into Two

    4.  **The Midpoint of Act I:** Cassian puts his hand on the door. He feels the vibration. *Joren’s voice screams.*

*   **Plants:** The Fossil Fields (visual horror).
*   **The Lie:** Cassian enters the "new world" of the deep past. He is committed.

## ACT II, PART 1: THE EXCAVATION (23% - 50%)

**Goal:** The journey into the deep layers. Fun and Games of Temporal Excavation. Cassian and Kait navigate the physical dangers of the Pit while dodging the Sanitizers. The relationship deepens.

### Chapter 7: The Fossil Field
*   **POV:** Cassian
*   **Location:** The Pit (Year 50 Strata)
*   **StC Beat:** Fun and Games
*   **% Mark:** 23-26%
*   **Emotional Arc:** Nauseated → Focused
*   **Try-Fail:** Yes-But (Cassian tunes his resonator to open the door, BUT the sound attracts the Sanitizers).
*   **Beats:**
    1.  Cassian sets up his "Tuning Fork" array. He needs to match the frequency of Year 50 to melt the Basalt.
    2.  Kait explores the fossils. She finds a child fossil. She is disturbed. "It's just rock, right?"
    3.  Cassian explains the cost. "That's a person. Compressed."
    4.  The resonance works. The door cracks open. But the sonic pulse acts as a beacon.
    5.  Searchlights sweep the chamber. The Sanitizers are deploying from the drill-head.
*   **Plants:** The child fossil (Kait’s fear of becoming history).
*   **The Lie:** Cassian focuses on the mechanics of the dig to ignore the moral weight.

### Chapter 8: The Queen’s Vault
*   **POV:** Cassian
*   **Location:** The Queen’s Vault
*   **StC Beat:** Fun and Games
*   **% Mark:** 26-30%
*   **Emotional Arc:** Reverence → Confusion
*   **Try-Fail:** Yes-And (They find the Queen, AND she is intact).
*   **Beats:**
    1.  Inside the vault. It is pristine, preserved by a stasis field (Pre-Scorch tech).
    2.  They find the Queen. She is not a fossil. She is in a glass coffin, looking like she is sleeping.
    3.  Cassian scans her. "She's... resonating. Her timeline is paused."
    4.  Kait tries to pick the lock on the coffin. "We can just take her and run."
    5.  Cassian stops her. "If we break the seal wrong, she shatters."
*   **Plants:** The glass coffin (stasis).
*   **The Lie:** Cassian treats her like an artifact, not a person.

### Chapter 9: The Ambush
*   **POV:** Cassian
*   **Location:** The Vault
*   **StC Beat:** Bad Guys Close In
*   **% Mark:** 30-34%
*   **Emotional Arc:** Panic → Desperation
*   **Try-Fail:** No-And (They try to hide, BUT the Sanitizers breach the vault).
*   **Beats:**
    1.  The vault door explodes. Sanitizer "Dredgers" (heavy infantry) enter.
    2.  Combat. Cassian uses a "Sledgehammer" spell — vibrating the floor to knock enemies over. High Nausea cost.
    3.  Kait is cornered. A Dredger raises a sonic lance.
    4.  Cassian tackles the Dredger. He takes a hit. His shoulder ages rapidly — the flesh withers to grey parchment in seconds.
    5.  They retreat deeper into the vault, sealing the blast door behind them. It won't hold long.
*   **Plants:** The aging injury (Cassian’s clock is ticking).
*   **The Lie:** Cassian sacrifices his body (history) for the mission.

### Chapter 10: The Extraction
*   **POV:** Cassian
*   **Location:** The Vault (Inner Sanctum)
*   **StC Beat:** Fun and Games / Midpoint
*   **% Mark:** 34-40%
*   **Emotional Arc:** Triumphant → Horrified
*   **Try-Fail:** Yes-But (Cassian successfully extracts the Queen, BUT she wakes up wrong).
*   **Beats:**
    1.  Trapped. Cassian realizes he has to wake her *now* or they die.
    2.  He hooks the stasis coffin to his own Chronometer. He uses his own life force to jumpstart her timeline.
    3.  **The Midpoint:** The glass cracks. The Queen awakes. She takes a breath.
    4.  She looks at Cassian. Her eyes are black voids. "Who disturbs the Silence?"
    5.  She touches Cassian’s wounded shoulder. The flesh *heals* but turns to stone. She is not just a woman; she is a temporal anomaly.
*   **Plants:** The Queen’s power (she can manipulate time, not just mine it).
*   **The Lie:** The "Want" achieved. He has the Queen. But she isn't what he expected.

### Chapter 11: The Tyrant’s Breath
*   **POV:** Cassian
*   **Location:** The Vault
*   **StC Beat:** Bad Guys Close In
*   **% Mark:** 40-45%
*   **Emotional Arc:** Subservient → Defiant
*   **Try-Fail:** No-But (The Queen tries to command Cassian, BUT he refuses to kneel).
*   **Beats:**
    1.  Queen Elara establishes dominance. She demands to know the year. When she hears "3000," she sneers. "Degenerates."
    2.  She views Kait as a servant. Kait bites the Queen's hand. The Queen laughs. "Fire in the ash. Good."
    3.  The blast door starts to buckle. The Sanitizers are cutting through.
    4.  Elara steps forward. She raises her hand. The air pressure drops. "I remember this frequency."
    5.  She rips a hole in the wall of the vault—a Time Rift. "Jump."
*   **Plants:** The Queen’s ruthlessness.
*   **The Lie:** Cassian realizes his "hero" is a monster.

### Chapter 12: The Rift
*   **POV:** Cassian
*   **Location:** The Time Rift (Between Layers)

    4.  They look around. It’s not the Slums. It’s the "Smog Layer" (Year 900). A toxic, yellow fog.

*   **The Lie:** Cassian is losing control of the "excavation."
