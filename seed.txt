A chronicler of illegal history must retrieve a dead queen from the geological layers of the city before the ruling Sanitizers erase her era entirely.

WORLD: Ouroboros, a city built vertically. The rich live at the top (The Present), which is constantly eroding and falling into The Pit (The Past). The poor live at the bottom, mining "Chronite" — a black, oily substance that is literally compressed time, harvested from the fossilized memories of citizens who lived centuries ago. The city smells of ozone and stale dust.

MAGIC/COST: Temporal Excavation. You can physically dig into the layers of the city to find moments that have been buried. If you dig up a specific moment of a person's life, you can relive it or steal their remaining years. The Cost is "Nausea" — a physical sickness where your own timeline begins to desynchronize, causing you to age rapidly or de-age into non-existence.

TENSION: The protagonist must excavate the Queen (a political revolutionary) from the deep strata before the "Sanitizers" drill through her layer to fuel the city's lights. If he brings her back, she will be a fossil — a woman out of time, brittle and dangerous. If he fails, the truth dies with her.

THEME: Is history a foundation we build on, or a resource we consume? The conflict between preserving the past and powering the present.
