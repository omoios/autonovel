## Geography
*   **World Name:** Ouroboros (SEED.TXT).
*   **Structure:** Vertical cylinder, roughly five miles in diameter, descending into the earth (WORLD.MD).
*   **The Present (Top):** The rich live here; constantly eroding and falling into The Pit (SEED.TXT).
*   **The Past (Bottom):** The poor live here; mining zone (SEED.TXT).
*   **The Spire:** The "Roof" of the cavern; location of The Present; smells of ozone, polished brass, and synthetic lavender; cool and thin air; blindingly bright electric lights (WORLD.MD).
*   **The Obsidian Eye:** A massive lens in the Spire ceiling that magnifies light from the surface world (scorching desert) down into the city (WORLD.MD).
*   **The Mid-Levels:** The middle band of the city; smells of frying grease, wet wool, and sewage; constant noise (WORLD.MD).
*   **The Grand Lifts:** Massive, clattering elevators in the Mid-Levels; dangerously overcrowded (WORLD.MD).
*   **The Slums (Near Past):** Just above the Pit; smells of heat, humidity, and rot; air thick with coal dust; dim reddish lighting (WORLD.MD).
*   **The Rust Belt:** A ring of derelict machinery hanging over the abyss in the Slums (WORLD.MD).
*   **The Pit (Deep Past):** Bottom of the cylinder; absolute silence broken by distant drills; smells of sulfur and old blood; pitch black; miners use green Chronite lamps (WORLD.MD).
*   **The Bore:** Massive central shaft in the Pit where Sanitizer drills grind into bedrock (WORLD.MD).
*   **The Basalt Threshold:** The deepest, hardest concrete layer; Queen Elara is buried here (WORLD.MD).
*   **The Mantle Limit:** The city is nearing this point; drills hit planet's core heat; lower levels becoming uninhabitable (WORLD.MD).
*   **Surface World:** A scorching desert (Solar Scorch); uninhabitable for 3000 years (WORLD.MD).
*   **Gravity:** Slightly higher in lower levels (closer to core); Spire visitors often faint/break bones (WORLD.MD).
*   **Light:** A commodity; brightness indicates status; stealing light is a capital crime (WORLD.MD).
*   **Sound:** Carries forever in compressed air; used for surveillance via acoustic pipes (WORLD.MD).

*   **The Founding (The Descent):** Occurred 3000 years ago; survivors burrowed into earth to find Chronite (WORLD.MD).

## Magic System Rules
*   **System Name:** Temporal Excavation (SEED.TXT).
*   **Mechanism:** Physical extraction of time from earth (mining) (WORLD.MD).
*   **Resource:** Chronite (black, oily substance; literally compressed time/fossilized memories) (SEED.TXT).
*   **Law of Viscosity:** Time behaves like a fluid; flows downward (Present to Past); cannot flow up (WORLD.MD).
*   **Law of Resonance:** To excavate, must match frequency of era using tuning fork or sonic drill (WORLD.MD).
*   **Law of Conservation:** Extracting memory creates a void; ground settles; too much extraction causes district collapse (WORLD.MD).
*   **Manifestation:** Excavated time is physical (black oil or geometric crystal); when burned, releases hallucination (WORLD.MD).
*   **The Cost:** "Nausea" (SEED.TXT).
*   **Nausea Mechanism:** Timeline desynchronization (SEED.TXT).
*   **Nausea Phase 1:** Vertigo, nosebleeds (black ichor) (WORLD.MD).
*   **Nausea Phase 2:** Rapid aging or de-aging (hair white/fall out, skin wrinkles/smooths) (WORLD.MD).
*   **Nausea Phase 3:** Existential Erasure (victim fades from reality; no memory remains; photos go blank) (WORLD.MD).
*   **Safe Limit:** Humans can safely excavate ~4 hours a day (WORLD.MD).
*   **Tools:** Chronometers (gauges measuring temporal drift) (WORLD.MD).
*   **Time Perception:** Moves slower in deep levels ("The Lag"); 1 week in Pit = 2 days in Spire (WORLD.MD).
*   **Cassian's Ability:** Master of Temporal Excavation; knows resonance frequencies of every era (CHARACTERS.MD).
*   **Cassian's Symptom:** "Chronos Shakes" (hands vibrate violently near deep time veins) (CHARACTERS.MD).
*   **Cassian's Tell:** Rubs left ear when lying (CHARACTERS.MD).
