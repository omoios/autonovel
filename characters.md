## VOICE IDENTITY (NOVEL SPECIFIC)

### Tone
The prose is **archival and urgent**. It reads like a salvage report written by a man who knows the ceiling is collapsing. The tone avoids melodrama; the horror is in the geology. The city is not a metaphor, it is a machine that eats people. The voice should feel cramped, dusty, and precise.

### Sentence Rhythm
*   **Narration:** Rigid and rhythmic. Sentences are short, clipped, factual. Subject-verb-object. The narration describes the physical world with the detachment of a geological survey, which makes the moments of human emotion land harder.
*   **Action:** Staccato. Fragments. Panic breaks the syntax.
*   **Description:** Accumulative. Lists of layers, smells, and textures to build density.

### Vocabulary Register
*   **Lexicon:** Industrial, geological, and archival. Words like *strata, sediment, excavation, compression, viscosity, era, residue, sanitization, audit*.
*   **Avoid:** Flowery euphemisms for death or magic. No "souls," "spirits," or "afterlife." Use *biomass*, *memory-echo*, *temporal displacement*.

### POV and Tense
**Third-Person Limited (The Chronicler).** We see only what Cassian sees. We know only what he remembers. The tense is **Immediate Past**. The narrative creates a claustrophobic intimacy by trapping us inside Cassian’s headspace, which is often addled by Nausea.

### Dialogue Conventions
*   **Tags:** Almost exclusively "said" or action beats. No adverbs in attribution.
*   **Style:** People in Ouroboros speak in shorthand. They are constantly listening for the sound of drilling or collapsing infrastructure. Conversations are quick, transactional, and paranoid. Subtext is king; saying the wrong word can attract a Sanitizer audit.

### Exemplar Passages
> The tunnel smelled of ozone and burnt hair. Cassian scraped the shovel against the black vein of Chronite, flaking away a chunk of compressed time. It hummed. A low-frequency vibration that rattled his teeth. The layer was dated Year 400. The Sanitation Era. If he dug too deep, he’d hit the bedrock of the Founding, and the pressure would crush his lungs like wet paper. He didn't dig for gold or jewels. He dug for the Queen. She was down there, fossilized in the shale, waiting for someone to crack her open and let the treason out.

> Above them, the city groaned. The sound of massive gears grinding against bedrock. The rich were awake in the Spire, polishing their marble, sweeping their dust into the chutes. It fell on the Slums like gray snow. "Hurry," the woman said. Her voice was thin, worn down by years of breathing recycled air. "The drill-bit is turning. I can feel the tremor in my knees." Cassian didn't answer. He just dug. The past was heavy, and it wanted to stay buried.

### Anti-Exemplars
> *The city was a tapestry of myriad wonders, a vibrant realm where the denizens wove their destinies into the very fabric of existence.* (Too flowery, too generic, violates the industrial tone).
> *He felt a magical connection to the ancient spirits of the earth.* (Too soft. Magic is physical and painful here).
> *The sun shone brightly on the bustling marketplace.* (There is no sun. The city is underground or encased in shadow. Light is a utility).

## CHARACTER REGISTRY

### 1. CASSIAN "CASS" VARR (The Chronicler)
**Age:** 34 (Biological), 52 (Chronological due to Nausea)
**Role:** Protagonist / Illegal Excavator

**THE THREE SLIDERS**
*   **Proactivity (8/10):** Cassian doesn't wait for the drills; he acts. He initiates the dig long before the Sanitizers arrive. His agency is the engine of the plot.
*   **Likability (7/10):** He is gruff, cynical, and physically repulsive (bleeds black ichor), but his devotion to saving history makes him sympathetic. He is a blue-collar hero.
*   **Competence (9/10):** He is a master of Temporal Excavation. He knows the resonance frequencies of every era. However, his competence is physically limited by his degrading body.

**GHOST / WOUND / LIE / WANT / NEED**
*   **Ghost:** The Day of the White Dust. Ten years ago, a Sanitizer drill breached a "toxic" timeline. Cassian’s team was exposed. He watched his brother, Joren, phase out of existence—edited from time while screaming for help that Cassian couldn't give.
*   **Wound:** **Survivor’s Guilt mixed with Temporal Nausea.** Cassian hears the "echo" of his brother’s voice in the silence of the deep strata. He is physically deteriorating, aging in jerky spurts, and knows his own timeline is short.
*   **Lie:** **"Memory is a solid."** Cassian believes that if he can just dig up the past and hold it, he can preserve it. He treats history like a rock to be collected rather than a fluid force that consumes him.
*   **Want:** To excavate the Queen’s fossil (The Basalt Threshold) and smuggle her into the Present before the Sanitizers drill through her layer. He wants to prove the "blank spot" in history is real.
*   **Need:** To accept that the past cannot be saved, only understood. He needs to stop digging for his brother in the rock and let go of the guilt that is aging him to death.

**ARC TYPE**
**Positive Tragedy.** Cassian will likely succeed in saving the Queen's memory but will likely succumb to the Nausea (existential erasure) in the process. He saves the truth but loses himself.

**SPEECH PATTERN (8 DIMENSIONS)**
1.  **Vocabulary:** Technical, terse. Uses geological metaphors for emotions ("I feel fractured," "The pressure is spiking").
2.  **Sentence Length:** Short. Declarative. Subject-verb-object.
3.  **Formality:** Low. Drogs consonants. Sounds like a tired man shouting over a drill.
4.  **Tics:** Rubs his left ear when lying (a nervous tick from the Nausea). Pauses mid-sentence to check his Chronometer.
5.  **Questions:** Rarely asks them. He answers questions or gives commands.
6.  **Interruptions:** Does not tolerate interruption. He talks over people to conserve oxygen/time.
7.  **Metaphor Domain:** **Excavation/Geology.** "That idea has no structural integrity." "She’s crumbling under the weight."
8.  **Directness:** High. 90% direct.

*Sample Dialogue:*
"The resonance is off. It's Year 50, but the vibration is... wrong. Like a scream in a library. Hand me the chisel. Not that one. The hardened steel. If you use the soft iron, the memory will bleed out and we'll both be drooling infants by sunrise."

**PHYSICALITY & HABITS**
*   **Appearance:** Gaunt, sallow skin. Has a patch of white hair at the temple (localized aging). His forearms are tattooed with black bars representing "Yields," but the bars are fading as his body rejects the ink.
*   **The Tell:** **Chronos Shakes.** When he is near a deep time vein, his hands vibrate violently. He wears heavy lead-weighted gloves to dampen the sensation.
*   **Habit:** Constantly checking his pocket watch. It doesn't tell time; it tells him how much "life" he has left before he desyncs.

**SECRETS**
1.  **The Hidden Archive:** Cassian didn't just excavate the Queen's location; he found a piece of *Pre-Scorch* technology in the deep strata and didn't turn it in. It's the only thing that might stop the Sanitizers' drill.
2.  **The Brother's Echo:** Cassian isn't just digging for the Queen. He's digging because he swears he can hear Joren’s voice coming from the Basalt Layer. He hopes the Queen is guarding his brother's lost timeline.

**RELATIONSHIPS**
*   **Vs. High Auditor Vex:** ideological opposites. Cassian preserves; Vex consumes.
*   **With Elara (The Queen):** Worshipper/Disciple. He views her as a martyr, but when he excavates her, he realizes she was a ruthless tyrant who did what was necessary.

### 2. HIGH AUDITOR VEX (The Antagonist)
**Age:** Unknown (appears 40)
**Role:** Head of the Sanitation Directorate / The Consumer

**THE THREE SLIDERS**
*   **Proactivity (9/10):** Vex is constantly expanding. He initiates the drilling of the Basalt Threshold. He creates the plot's urgency.
*   **Likability (2/10):** Cold, clinical, arrogant. He views people as "biomass." However, he is polite and soft-spoken, which makes him chillingly effective.
*   **Competence (10/10):** He runs the city's energy grid. He understands the physics of Chronite better than anyone. He is a terrifying administrator.

**GHOST / WOUND / LIE / WANT / NEED**
*   **Ghost:** **The Blackout.** As a child in the Mid-Levels, Vex lived through a year where the Chronite ration was cut. He watched his family freeze in the dark. He swore he would never be cold again.
*   **Wound:** **Thermal PTSD.** He is obsessed with heat and light. He keeps his office sweltering hot. He fears the dark (the Past).
*   **Lie:** **"The Past is a Resource, not a grave."** He justifies mining human history by claiming it "honors" the dead by giving their energy to the living. He refuses to admit he is burning his ancestors.
*   **Want:** To breach the Basalt Threshold and extract the "Primary Vein" of Chronite beneath the Queen. This will fuel the city for a thousand years and cement his legacy.
*   **Need:** To realize that consuming the past destroys the future. The city is running out of vertical space because he is cannibalizing the foundation.

**ARC TYPE**
**Negative/Static.** He refuses to repent. He would rather burn the world than admit he was wrong. He represents the system that cannot be reasoned with, only broken or bypassed.

**SPEECH PATTERN (8 DIMENSIONS)**
1.  **Vocabulary:** Latinate, bureaucratic, sterile. Uses words like "efficiency," "reclamation," "audit," "biomass."
2.  **Sentence Length:** Long, flowing, complex sentences. He talks in paragraphs.
3.  **Formality:** High. He never uses contractions. He speaks with the detachment of a surgeon.
4.  **Tics:** Cleans his porcelain faceplate with a silk handkerchief when stressed.
5.  **Questions:** Rhetorical. He asks questions he already knows the answer to.
6.  **Interruptions:** Never interrupts, but silences others with a raised hand.
7.  **Metaphor Domain:** **Medicine/Hygiene.** "The infection of history," "amputate the era," "sterilize the strata."
8.  **Directness:** Low. He speaks in euphemisms to hide the horror of his actions.

*Sample Dialogue:*
"We do not destroy, Mr. Varr. We liberate. The energy trapped within that shale is dormant, useless. A waste of potential. By processing the biomass of the Third Era, we are granting those long-dead citizens a purpose they never achieved in life. We are elevating them from mere memory to utility. Now, step away from the drill. The audit is complete."

**PHYSICALITY & HABITS**
*   **Appearance:** Wears a pristine white uniform. His face is covered in a porcelain mask (common for high-ranking Sanitizers) to hide the "rot"—cancerous sores caused by handling too much raw Chronite. Only his eyes are visible, and they are pale, watery blue.
*   **The Tell:** **The Finger Tap.** When he is angry, he taps his index finger against his porcelain cheek. The hollow *clack-clack-clack* sound terrifies his subordinates.
*   **Habit:** Constantly adjusting the thermostat. He is always cold.

**SECRETS**
1.  **The Porcelain Rot:** Vex isn't just hiding scars; his face is gone. He has no lower jaw. It rotted off years ago from Chronite poisoning. He speaks through a vocal synthesizer in his throat.
2.  **The Vein is Dry:** Vex knows the Primary Vein is running dry. The drilling is a sham to keep the populace calm while he secures a VIP escape pod to the surface. He is looting the city on his way out.

**RELATIONSHIPS**
*   **Vs. Cassian:** Vex sees Cassian as a "hoarder," a dirty child refusing to throw away a broken toy.
*   **With The Silentium:** He hunts them ruthlessly because they represent the chaos he fears.

### 3. MOTHER OTHO (The Mentor)
**Age:** 24 (Biological), 80 (Chronological)
**Role:** Leader of The Excavators / Cell Commander

**THE THREE SLIDERS**
*   **Proactivity (7/10):** She organizes the resistance. She moves people and resources. She is the general.
*   **Likability (9/10):** Nurturing but tough. The "Mom" of the revolution. Everyone wants to protect her, but she protects them.
*   **Competence (6/10):** She is a brilliant organizer but physically weak. Her Nausea is advanced; she cannot dig anymore.

**GHOST / WOUND / LIE / WANT / NEED**
*   **Ghost:** **The Orphanage.** Otho was a "Duster" (street child) recruited by the previous Chronicler. She watched the previous generation of Excavators get wiped out by a Sanitizer gas attack.
*   **Wound:** **Accelerated Aging.** She looks like a withered old woman, but she is only twenty-four. Her body is failing.
*   **Lie:** **"I am already dead."** She treats her life as disposable because she believes she has no future. She sends young people to die because she thinks she has already lived her life.
*   **Want:** To preserve the Queen’s knowledge (the "Blueprints") so the city can stop sinking.
*   **Need:** To accept that she deserves a future, and that sacrificing herself isn't noble—it's a tragedy.

**ARC TYPE**
**Sacrificial Matron.** She will likely die holding the line so Cassian can escape with the Queen.

**SPEECH PATTERN (8 DIMENSIONS)**
1.  **Vocabulary:** Maternal, rough, affectionate. Uses terms of endearment ("child," "love," "bird").
2.  **Sentence Length:** Variable. She rambles when storytelling, snaps orders when in crisis.
3.  **Formality:** Low. She touches people when she speaks.
4.  **Tics:** Coughs wetly into a handkerchief that comes away black.
5.  **Questions:** Constantly checking on status. "You eat? You sleep?"
6.  **Interruptions:** Interrupts to care for people. "Don't give me that look. Eat."
7.  **Metaphor Domain:** **Domestic/Textile.** "Weaving," "patching," "knots."
8.  **Directness:** High. She tells hard truths wrapped in kindness.

*Sample Dialogue:*
"Look at you. Skinny as a lamp-vine root and twice as brittle. You haven't been sleeping, the tremors are back. Sit. I’ve got broth—real broth, not the slurry. The world can end after you’ve eaten. I won’t have you fainting in the tunnel, Cassian. I’m too old to carry you back."

**PHYSICALITY & HABITS**
*   **Appearance:** Hunched back, wrinkled skin, thinning gray hair. She wears a shawl made of scavenged electric blankets to stay warm.
*   **The Tell:** **The Knuckle Crack.** She cracks her knuckles constantly. It's the only thing that relieves the arthritis pain caused by rapid aging.
*   **Habit:** Collecting "non-essential" items (buttons, colored glass). She hides beauty in a world of utility.

**SECRETS**
1.  **The Queen's Lineage:** Otho is a direct descendant of the Queen’s handmaidens. The "stories" she tells are actually family secrets passed down.
2.  **The Betrayal:** She knows where the Sanitizer base is weak because she sold them supplies (food) last winter to feed her orphans. She is compromised.

### 4. THE QUEEN ELARA (The Fossil / The MacGuffin)
**Age:** 3000 (Deceased)
**Role:** The Revolutionary / The Object

**THE THREE SLIDERS**
*   **Proactivity (0/10 initially -> 10/10 when awoken):** She is a statue for most of the book. When she wakes, she takes command immediately.
*   **Likability (5/10):** She is commanding, severe, and ruthless. She is not a "nice" person. She is a Queen.
*   **Competence (10/10):** She ruled the city. She understands the geology of Ouroboros better than any modern engineer.

**GHOST / WOUND / LIE / WANT / NEED**
*   **Ghost:** **The Solar Scorch.** She remembers the surface. She remembers the sun.
*   **Wound:** **The Silence.** She was buried alive in concrete, screaming, while her city turned against her.
*   **Lie:** **"I can save everyone."** She believed she could stop the mining without destroying the city's economy. She couldn't.
*   **Want:** To return to the surface (The Descent Reversed).
*   **Need:** To realize her time is over. She is a ghost haunting the machine.

**ARC TYPE**
**The Relic.** She is a force of nature. When awoken, she is terrifying. She views the modern citizens (Cassian, Otho) as "degenerate dwarves" living in her ruin.

**SPEECH PATTERN (8 DIMENSIONS)**
1.  **Vocabulary:** Archaic, courtly, imperious. Uses "thee/thou" or formal "High Speech."
2.  **Sentence Length:** Long, commanding sentences.
3.  **Formality:** Extreme. She does not slang.
4.  **Tics:** Stares unblinkingly. She is still getting used to having eyes again.
5.  **Questions:** Interrogative. "Who dares?" "What year is this?"
6.  **Interruptions:** Does not allow interruption.
7.  **Metaphor Domain:** **Light/Architecture.** "Foundations," "illumination," "monuments."
8.  **Directness:** Absolute.

*Sample Dialogue:*
"Silence. The air... it tastes of ash and recycled death. Is this what my city has become? A tomb burrowing into the guts of the earth? You there, miner. You look upon me with fear. Good. It means you still remember what reverence feels like. Speak. What year of the Interregnum is it?"

**PHYSICALITY & HABITS**
*   **Appearance:** When excavated, she looks like a statue made of black basalt and gold veins. As she "thaws," she becomes a woman of terrifying beauty—sharp features, eyes like obsidian.
*   **The Tell:** **The Pulse of the City.** She doesn't walk; she flows. She seems to know when the city groans before it happens.
*   **Habit:** Touching walls. She is re-memorizing her city.

**SECRETS**
1.  **The Weapon:** She didn't stop the mining to save history. She stopped it because they were digging too close to **The Mantle**—and something was waking up down there. She isn't a savior; she’s a jailer.

**RELATIONSHIPS**
*   **With Cassian:** She views him as a tool, then a subject, then finally, a worthy successor.
*   **With Vex:** She remembers his ancestor. She knows the weakness of the Sanitizers.

### 5. KAIT (The Wildcard / The Duster)
**Age:** 12
**Role:** Guide / Thief / The Future

**THE THREE SLIDERS**
*   **Proactivity (9/10):** Kait acts first and thinks never. She steals, climbs, and explores constantly.
*   **Likability (8/10):** Scrappy, funny, brave. The audience surrogate for the wonder/horror of the city.
*   **Competence (7/10):** She can fit into vents and climb walls adults can't. She knows the slums. She can't fight, but she can run.

**GHOST / WOUND / LIE / WANT / NEED**
*   **Ghost:** **The Drop.** Her mother fell off the Rust Belt into the Pit when Kait was 5.
*   **Wound:** **Abandonment.** She trusts no one.
*   **Lie:** **"I don't need anyone."**
*   **Want:** To find enough Chronite to buy a ticket to the Spire (The Present).
*   **Need:** A family. She bonds with Cassian and Otho.

**ARC TYPE**
**Coming of Age.** She learns that the Spire is a lie and that her family is the people she digs with.

**SPEECH PATTERN (8 DIMENSIONS)**
1.  **Vocabulary:** Street slang, fast, high-pitched.
2.  **Sentence Length:** Run-on sentences. Excited.
3.  **Formality:** None. Uses nicknames for everyone.
4.  **Tics:** Whistles when nervous. Chews on copper wire.
5.  **Questions:** "Why?" "What's that?" "Can I have it?"
6.  **Interruptions:** Constantly.
7.  **Metaphor Domain:** **Vermin/Scavenging.** "Rat-trap," "shiny," "crumbs."
8.  **Directness:** Brutally honest.

*Sample Dialogue:*
"Oi, Old Man! You dropped your glow-bulb. S'broken now. Shame. Hey, you think the Queen has jewels in her pockets? Real ones? Not the glass ones from the market. If we find her, I get first dibs on her crown, deal? Deal. You’re too slow, keep up!"

**PHYSICALITY & HABITS**
*   **Appearance:** Tiny, wears oversized goggles and a coat made of sewn-together rags. Dirty face, bright eyes.
*   **The Tell:** **The Twitch.** She is always moving. Tapping foot, bouncing on heels.
*   **Habit:** Stealing small things and putting them back. Just to prove she can.

**SECRETS**
1.  **The Rat King:** She talks to the Rock-Rats. They bring her information from the vents. She knows the Sanitizers are moving ahead of schedule because the rats told her.

**RELATIONSHIPS**
*   **With Cassian:** Annoying little sister / Protegee.
*   **With The Queen:** The only person who isn't scared of her. Kait sees Elara as just another "rich lady" to rob.

Joren appears to Cassian during high-Nausea episodes. He is the physical manifestation of Cassian's guilt. Cassian must "excavate" his brother's memory from the rock to say goodbye, or he will succumb to the Nausea and join him.

**SPEECH PATTERN**

*   **Cassian & Vex:** Thesis (Preservation) vs. Antithesis (Consumption).
*   **Cassian & Otho:** Father/Daughter dynamic (reversed ages). She is the wisdom, he is the muscle.
*   **Cassian & Kait:** Protector/Apprentice. She represents the future he is fighting for.
*   **Cassian & The Queen:** Worshipper/Deity -> Equals.
*   **Cassian & Joren:** The Living vs. The Dead. The central emotional conflict.
*   **Vex & The Queen:** Usurper and Monarch. Vex is living in the house she built, trying to tear it down for firewood.
